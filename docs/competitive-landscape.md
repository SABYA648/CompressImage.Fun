# Competitive landscape

Checked 22 August 2026 against official pages listed in `docs/seo-research-log.md`. No rankings, search volumes, or unverified prices are asserted.

Archetypes:

- **Upload compressors** (TinyPNG, iLoveIMG, FreeConvert, ShortPixel, CloudConvert image tools) trade operational consistency and format breadth for a server copy of the file.
- **Browser codec labs** (Squoosh, plus 2026 exact-KB WASM tools such as ShrinkTo) keep bytes on device and often lack native HEIC/batch/VPS-bounded jobs.
- **Exact-KB SERP tools** sell “type 50 KB” plus, too often, government presets and “no quality loss.”

compressimage.fun’s defensible combination is:

1. Measured exact-size search (quality first, then dimensions).
2. Batch ZIP without signup or product watermark.
3. Honest format coverage, including animation rejection.
4. Form-photo preparation without fake compliance.
5. A labeled hybrid: Sharp on the server, Base64/PDF/color in the browser.

We do not claim to be “no upload like Squoosh” on the compressor, and we do not claim TinyPNG’s or iLoveIMG’s current quotas as ours.
